Server rules

Short list, easy to follow. They exist so the work people put into their builds doesn't get wrecked by someone else.

01

No griefing

Do not intentionally destroy or damage another player's builds.

02

No stealing

Do not take items from other players without permission.

03

No cheating

Do not use hacked clients, unfair exploits, or other cheats that give you an unfair advantage.

04

Respect other players

Treat other members fairly and do not harass, threaten, or intentionally target people.

05

No spam

Do not spam the chat or repeatedly send unnecessary messages.

06

No advertising

Do not advertise unrelated servers, communities, websites, or services without permission.

07

No NSFW content

Keep the server appropriate for its community.

08

Follow staff instructions

Staff decisions and instructions should be respected.

09

Do not abuse bugs

If you discover a serious exploit or bug, report it instead of abusing it.

10

Use common sense

Not every possible situation can be listed here. Staff may act on behaviour that clearly harms the server or its community.

!

These rules can change. They get updated as the server does, and you're responsible for checking the current version here. Updates are posted in Discord too.
