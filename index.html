Spawn

A valley built around the CRIX monument, with NPCs, crates and floating leaderboards. /rtp drops you on solid ground in the Overworld, Nether or End.

/shop

Shop and sell

Buy from 300+ items in 15 categories. /sell opens a chest: drop items in, close it and you're paid. One hot item pays 25% extra each hour.

/shop · /sell

/ah

Auction house

List items for 48 hours with a 5% tax, or post a buy order and the money is held until someone fills it. /quickbuy grabs the cheapest listing.

/ah · /orders

Crates

Crates

Five crates at spawn

Tap a crate at spawn to see every reward and its chance, or sneak and tap to open one straight away. Keys come from the free hourly key, /daily and the shard shop.

Common Crate

Enchanted diamond armor, a Sharpness IV sword and an Efficiency IV pickaxe, axe or shovel.

KeyFree every hour, or 200 shards

Gold Crate

One mob spawner: Pig, Cow, Zombie, Skeleton, Spider, Blaze, or an Iron Golem (4%).

Key1,500 shards, or day 5 of /daily

Amethyst Crate

An amethyst pickaxe or shovel that digs 3×3, a tree-felling axe (they break after 3 days), Haste II brews or amethyst shards.

Key3,000 shards

Prime Crate

Prime netherite armor, a Sharpness V sword, a Density V mace, a crossbow, or the Drill (8.7%).

Key2,000 shards

The top crate

Crimson Crate

A piece of the Crimson armor set, or a Crimson sword (Sharpness V), pickaxe (Efficiency V, Fortune III) or axe.

Rare prize: the CRIX Chestplate, a 4.5% chance on every key.

Key2,500 shards, or day 7 of /daily
