      Questions

      Before you join

      Is it free to play?

      Yes. Anyone with Minecraft Bedrock can join the Realm with the code. Ranks, gear and keys are all earned in game.

      Can I play on my phone or console?

      Yes. The Realm works on every device that runs Minecraft Bedrock: phone, tablet, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation and Switch. Consoles may need their online membership to play online.

      Why does Minecraft download a pack when I join?

      That's the CRIX resource pack. It adds the CRIX menus, items and the sidebar, and downloads by itself when you join.

      Is there PvP at spawn?

      No. PvP is off at spawn and in the AFK room, on in the survival world, and duels happen in their own arenas.

      What happens if I log out in a fight?

      Hitting or being hit tags you for 15 seconds. Log out while tagged and you drop everything where you stood, and your opponent gets the kill.

      How do I protect my base?

      Claim chunks with /claim. Everyone gets 4 chunks, ranks add up to 16 more, and /trust lets your friends build there.

      Can I buy a rank?

      No. Ranks cost in-game money and playtime. When you have both, type /rankup.

      Can I go to the End?

      Yes, but the main island is off limits. /rtp end takes you to a random outer island.
